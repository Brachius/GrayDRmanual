# Reconnect Recovery

Script name: `reconnect-recovery`

Put a reconnected character back in order before Master Train resumes.

## How to start it

Type `.reconnect-recovery` followed by the words it takes.

No arguments.

In Master Train, pick **Reconnect recovery** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
