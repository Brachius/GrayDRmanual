# Tinkering

Script name: `tinker`

Native port of tinker.lic including mechanisms and bow enhancements.

## How to start it

Type `.tinker` followed by the words it takes.

chapter recipe material noun; resume/instructions/enhancement/mechanisms forms

Example:

```
.tinker 7 "wooden bolt" maple bolts
```

In Master Train, pick **Tinkering** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
