# repair-dropoff

Script name: `repair-dropoff`

Drops worn armour and shield with the repairer by REPAIR ALL, hands over every other gear item that is not marked never for repair, and stows every ticket. It does not wait for the repairs. Money: starts with 100 platinum, tops up 20 when the repairer needs more, finishes at the bank with 20 platinum.

## How to start it

Type `.repair-dropoff` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
