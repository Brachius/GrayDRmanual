# Enchanting

Script name: `enchant`

Native port of enchant.lic with sigil/fount/imbue handling.

## How to start it

Type `.enchant` followed by the words it takes.

chapter recipe noun [base noun], or resume noun

Example:

```
.enchant 1 "radiant trinket" totem
```

In Master Train, pick **Enchanting** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

On the Configuration Studio page [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md):

| Setting | What it does | Default | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enchanting room override** | A number, at least 0. Must be a room on the map. | 0 | Global, or each character |
