# kiosk

Script name: `kiosk`

The Trader kiosk. `.kiosk` makes sure the kiosk is up in the kiosk room (summons or refreshes it) with the markup set; `.kiosk maintain` refreshes it when it is close to expiring; `.kiosk service` takes everything it holds, tops the reserve up from the bank and sells the crystalline bundles at the furrier; `.kiosk status` prints CONC and STUDY and changes nothing. Settings are on the Trading Training page under Trader kiosk. The tessera leaves its container only for the cast and for POKE, TAP and TOUCH, and goes straight back.

## How to start it

Type `.kiosk` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
