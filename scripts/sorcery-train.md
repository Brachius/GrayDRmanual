# sorcery-train

Script name: `sorcery-train`

Trains Sorcery with the Sorcery entry under Training spells on the Magic page, the same way utility-train, ward-train and aug-train train their skills. Stops at mind lock.

## How to start it

Type `.sorcery-train` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
