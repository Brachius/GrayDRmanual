# Shaping

Script name: `shape`

Shape wood items exactly like shape.lic, including consumable restocking by chapter, assembly parts, and bow enhancements.

## How to start it

Type `.shape` followed by the words it takes.

hold|log|stow|trash + chapter/recipe/material/noun [skip], or instructions form, laminate/lighten/cable &lt;noun&gt;, or resume &lt;noun&gt;.

Example:

```
.shape log 7 "comb" maple comb
```

In Master Train, pick **Shaping (Shape)** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

On the Configuration Studio page [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md):

| Setting | What it does | Default | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Private engineering room (0 = public shaping rooms)** | A number, at least 0. Must be a room on the map. | 0 | Global, or each character |
