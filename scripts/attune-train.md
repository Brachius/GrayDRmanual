# Attunement Training

Script name: `attune-train`

Mind locks Attunement by walking the concentration rooms in turn and perceiving in each. Add `sacrifice` to allow the sacrifice step.

## How to start it

Type `.attune-train` in the game window.

In Master Train, pick **Attunement training** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
