# plat

Script name: `plat`

Money converter. `.plat 30 dokoras` shows that amount in copper and what it is worth in Kronars and Lirums. The currency word can be shortened to its first three letters. Sends nothing to the game.

## How to start it

Type `.plat` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
