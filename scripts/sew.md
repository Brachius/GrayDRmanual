# Tailoring

Script name: `sew`

Sew, knit, or enhance tailoring items exactly like sew.lic, including consumable restocking and rental renewal.

## How to start it

Type `.sew` followed by the words it takes.

hold|log|stow|trash + type/chapter/recipe/material/noun, or instructions form, seal/reinforce/lighten, or resume &lt;noun&gt;.

Example:

```
.sew log sewing 2 "heavy gloves" wool gloves
```

In Master Train, pick **Tailoring (Sew)** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
