# Deed Sorting (Hollow Eve)

Script name: `deed-sort`

The Hollow Eve deed-sorting party. Everyone stands in one room with their sack deeds in the duffel bag and runs it (`.deed-sort cascade` starts it on every character at once). In name order each one takes a turn as the giver: for each material family in the Data Editor's Zasele keep list, the deeds come out of the duffel bag and go to that family's keeper, while everyone else accepts what is offered and puts it in their bag. The giver nods to the next name; the last giver nods to everyone and it ends. A keeper who is not in the room keeps nothing.

`.deed-sort tidy`, at a Society hall, rebuilds everything in your duffel bag plus the deeds already in your register into the fewest full-size deeds, puts them in the register, and writes the full ones up as "N/N". Metal needs the crucible room (the pieces are melted together with Smelt); wood, leather and cloth work in any hall room.

## How to start it

Type `.deed-sort` followed by the words it takes.

nothing for the party, or tidy.

Example:

```
.deed-sort tidy
```

In Master Train, pick **Deed Sorting** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.
