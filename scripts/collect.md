# collect

Script name: `collect`

Walks to the collecting room and collects rocks until both Outdoorsmanship and Perception are done learning: mind locked, or at their rank cap.

## How to start it

Type `.collect` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
