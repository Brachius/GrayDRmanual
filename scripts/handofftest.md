# handofftest

Script name: `handofftest`

A real handoff with the receiver, on demand, from an idle giver: full pouches from the configured container, the scroll chest, and the boxes, exactly as Master Train's own handoff does it. `.handofftest pouches`, `scrolls` or `boxes` limits it to those kinds; no word means all three. A receiver running Master Train does his side at his next safe moment; an idle receiver gets his receiver script started for him.

## How to start it

Type `.handofftest` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
