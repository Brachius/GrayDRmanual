# Spin Thread

Script name: `spin`

Buys fibers at the tailoring society and spins them into one thread item on the society's spinning wheel.

```
.spin silk
.spin burlap heavy
```

The material is silk, cotton, linen, wool or burlap. The second word sets the wheel: fine, thin, average, thick, heavy (the default) or yarn.

## How to start it

Type `.spin` with the words shown above.

Words it understands: `silk`, `cotton`, `linen`, `wool`, `burlap`.

In Master Train, pick **Spin Thread** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
