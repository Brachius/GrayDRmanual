# hospital-heal

Script name: `hospital-heal`

Heals at the Empath's plant room when one is configured on the Healing page, otherwise at Quentin's hospital. `.hospital-heal force` runs the whole visit even with no injuries found.

## How to start it

Type `.hospital-heal` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
