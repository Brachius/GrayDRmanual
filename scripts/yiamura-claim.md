# Yiamura Claim

Script name: `yiamura-claim`

Collect the knowledge the yiamura's observer gathered during a hunt (OBSERVE MY YIAMURA). Does nothing when there is nothing to claim.

## How to start it

Type `.yiamura-claim` in the game window.

In Master Train, pick **Yiamura Claim** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](../studio/character.md), [Magic](../studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](../studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
