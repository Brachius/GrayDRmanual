# Scroll Manage

Script name: `scroll-manage`

Sort loose spell scrolls from the backpack and the chest in the backpack into the two eternity scroll folios, exactly like the custom scroll-manage Lich script. Runs after a handoff hands Tirax scrolls.

## How to start it

Type `.scroll-manage` followed by the words it takes.

No arguments. The word help shows the usage.

In Master Train, pick **Scroll Manage** as the activity and put the same words in its Arguments box.

## Settings

On the Configuration Studio page [Magic](../studio/magic.md):

| Setting | What it does | Default | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scroll spells to keep** | One spell per row, by its exact name or its abbreviation (for example Ethereal Shield or ES). A scroll for a spell in this list is stacked in the white folio, then the black folio when the white one is full. Every other scroll goes in the bucket. An empty list refuses to sort at all, so nothing is thrown away by accident. A list. | (not set) | Global, or each character |
