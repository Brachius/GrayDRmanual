# autofavor

Script name: `autofavor`

Favors in a loop. `.autofavor 30` runs until FAVOR reports 30: an orb from the temple puzzle, rubbed until the game says the sacrifice is properly prepared, then put on the town's favor altar. When a rub says the pools are empty, Utility magic trains to mind lock in the magic training room and the rubbing resumes. `.autofavor drain` is one orb only: rub with the experience on hand, turn it in if it fills, otherwise stow it and stop.

## How to start it

Type `.autofavor` in the game window.

## Settings

This script has no settings of its own. Shared pages such as [Character](/studio/character.md), [Magic](/studio/magic.md) and [Crafting](/studio/crafting.md) may still apply.
