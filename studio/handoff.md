# Handoff

Configuration Studio page **Handoff**. Open it from the left-hand list; type part of a setting's name in the search box to find it.

The gem-pouch and item exchange between a giver and the receiver, Jang: how many full pouches and boxes trigger it, the F2P platinum rules, and who takes part.

| Setting | What it does | Default | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full pouches before handoff** | On a giver, request a handoff after a completed hunt when this many full hunting or box-picking pouches are stored. Leave blank to disable giving. A number, at least 1. | (not set) | Each character |
| **Jewelry and keepsake boxes before handoff** | On a giver, also request a handoff after a completed hunt when this many jewelry and keepsake boxes together (any mix) are in the full pouch container. Leave blank to count pouches only. A number, at least 1. | 5 | Each character |
| **F2P: give excess platinum to the receiver** | After the pouches, the giver hands Jang its platinum Dokoras above Platinum to keep, in one GIVE. Free-to-play allows one coin handoff per cooldown; when the game refuses with its handoff-limit line the platinum is kept and the handoff goes on. Dokoras only; gold, silver and copper stay. Yes or No. | No | Each character |
| **F2P: platinum to keep** | How many platinum Dokoras the giver keeps on hand; everything above it goes to Jang. A number, at least 0. | 20 | Each character |
| **Handoff participants** | Jang's fixed handoff order. Only Jang needs this list. A list. | (not set) | Each character |
